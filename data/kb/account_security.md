# Account and Security

## Resetting two-factor authentication
To reset two-factor authentication, an account owner opens Settings, then Security, then Reset 2FA. We email a one-time recovery link that is valid for 30 minutes. Support staff can never disable two-factor authentication on request.

## Single sign-on
Single sign-on with SAML 2.0 is available on the Business and Enterprise plans. Administrators configure it under Admin Console, then Identity.

## Data residency
Enterprise customers can pin stored data to the EU or US region. Changing region after onboarding requires a migration and takes about 3 business days.
