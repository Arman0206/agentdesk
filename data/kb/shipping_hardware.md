# Hardware Shipping

## Delivery times
Nimbus Hub devices ship within 2 business days. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days, and express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days.

## Tracking
A tracking number is emailed once the order status changes to shipped. Orders marked processing have not left the warehouse yet.

## Damaged on arrival
If a device arrives damaged, report it within 7 days with a photo. We send a free replacement and cover return shipping.
