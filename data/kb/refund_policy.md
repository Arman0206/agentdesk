# Refund Policy

## Annual plans
Annual subscriptions can be refunded in full within 14 days of the purchase date. After 14 days, refunds are prorated for the unused full months only.

## Monthly plans
Monthly subscriptions are not refunded once the billing cycle has started. You can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the paid period.

## Processing and approval
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. Refunds above 500 USD require manager approval and may take up to 10 business days.

## Hardware
The Nimbus Hub device can be returned within 30 days in its original packaging for a full refund. The customer pays return shipping unless the device arrived damaged.
